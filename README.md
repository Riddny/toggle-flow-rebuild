# Growth Audit — Toggle VPN

**Live:** https://riddny.github.io/toggle-flow-rebuild/
**Prototype only:** https://riddny.github.io/toggle-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

Five ranked ideas for Toggle VPN's iOS onboarding and paywall, each built into a clickable prototype in Toggle's own design. The screens are yours: the near-black surfaces, the green, the pill plan cards, the wall of streaming logos and the toggle switch. Only the order changes, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give a second chance when they close the paywall: your $11.99 monthly plan, billed today, no trial | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Ask what they need a VPN for and how many devices, then read it back | Use · Devices · Loader · Payoff | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | Put their reason in the paywall headline | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Show your 600K+ installs and a review before the price | Payoff | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Put the 3-day trial on the yearly plan you recommend, with the charge date | Paywall | CR +5–15% |

The prototype has a Light / Dark switch (dark is Toggle's own; light is our approximation), English / Spanish / German, a 30-second walkthrough, and jump chips that land on any screen with answers filled in.

**Grounding.** Every price, figure, review and feature line comes from Toggle VPN's own App Store listing, website (toggle.org, toggle.org/pricing) and in-app screens. Reviews are quoted word for word. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Toggle VPN.
